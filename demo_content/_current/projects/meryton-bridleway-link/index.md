Riders and cyclists using the bridleways near Meryton currently have to ride
along a fast, narrow road to get from one to the other. Lydia Bennet and
Colonel Fitzwilliam are working with landowners, including Charles Bingley,
to explore an off-road link.

The map shows the existing bridleways in the area and the points where the
link could join them.
