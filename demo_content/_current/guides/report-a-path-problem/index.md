---
title: How to report a problem on a path
summary: What to note down, and who to tell, when a path is blocked or damaged.
---
We cannot deal with individual problems on a path. They are reported to the
highway authority, which is usually the county council.

## Before you report

Note down:

- **Where** the problem is. Use the path number or name if you know it, the
  nearest village or landmark, and a map reference or the coordinates from a
  phone map app.
- **What** is wrong, for example a broken stile, a fallen tree, a missing sign,
  crops over the path or an overgrown hedge.
- **When** you saw it.

A photo helps. Take one that shows the problem and one that shows where it is.

## Making your report

1. Find your highway authority's page for reporting a rights of way problem.
   Search for "report a public right of way problem" and the name of your
   county.
2. Fill in the online form, or email or phone if you prefer.
3. Keep the reference number you are given.

## After you report

The authority decides how urgent the problem is. A blocked path or a dangerous
structure is usually dealt with sooner than a faded waymark. If you hear
nothing, quote your reference number and ask for an update.

If the problem affects many people or has lasted a long time, you can also
[contact us](/contact/). We cannot fix it ourselves, but we can raise it with
the authority.

See the [glossary](/glossary/) for terms such as *highway authority* and
*path furniture*.
