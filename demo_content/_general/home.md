Local Access Forums are independent advisory groups that help make the
countryside easier for everyone to reach and enjoy. We advise on public rights
of way, open access land and other routes used by walkers, horse riders,
cyclists, carriage drivers and people with limited mobility.

This site brings together our [projects](/projects/), a [map](/map/) of public
rights of way, [how-to guides](/guides/), a [glossary](/glossary/) of
access terms, and links to [resources](/resources/) from other organisations.

*This is a demonstration site. The forum and its projects are fictional, with
names borrowed from Pride and Prejudice. The rights of way shown on the
[map](/map/) are real.*
