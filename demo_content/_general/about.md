## What we do

Local Access Forums were set up under section 94 of the Countryside and Rights
of Way Act 2000. Each forum gives independent advice on improving public access
to land for open-air recreation and enjoyment.

We advise:

- the county council, as the highway authority responsible for public rights
  of way
- other public bodies, such as district and parish councils
- landowners, land managers and user groups, when asked

## Who we are

Our members are volunteers who bring different interests and experience:
walkers, horse riders, cyclists, farmers, landowners, parish councils, and
people who know the barriers faced by those with limited mobility. See
[our members](/members/).

## How we work

- We hold a full forum [meeting](/meetings/) four times a year. Meetings are
  open to the public, and agendas and minutes are published here.
- Small groups of members take on specific [projects](/projects/), such as
  surveying a parish's paths or reviewing a proposed new route.
- We respond to consultations on matters affecting public access.

## What we do not do

We cannot deal with individual problems on a path, such as a blocked stile or
an overgrown hedge. Please report these directly to the county council.
