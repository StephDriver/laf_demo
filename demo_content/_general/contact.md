## Reporting a problem on a path

We cannot deal with individual problems on a path, such as a blocked stile, a
missing sign or an overgrown hedge. Please report these to the county council,
which is responsible for maintaining public rights of way.

## Attending a meeting

Our [meetings](/meetings/) are open to the public. Please get in touch at least
a week beforehand if you would like to attend or ask a question.
