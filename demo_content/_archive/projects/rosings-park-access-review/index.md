title: Rosings Park Access Review
summary: A review of permissive access around a large private park, completed in 2025.
started: "2024-09"
ended: "2025-09"
---
This project reviewed the permissive paths around Rosings Park, at the
invitation of its owner, Lady Catherine de Bourgh. The review recommended
clearer signs at each entrance and a new permissive path along the lake.

The project is now complete. Its final report was presented to the forum in
September 2025.
